THE NEW PROBLEM

People and AI are working in silos

AI is already powerful. The way we use it leaves most of that power on the table.

No big picture

Nobody, human or AI, sees the whole project. Duplicate work, conflicts and gaps are routine.

No shared process

Everyone uses AI their own way, and the work never connects.

No common quality bar

Quality varies from person to person and is judged by gut feel.

No paper trail

Nobody can audit or trace what was delivered, or how.
