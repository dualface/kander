312: - `dispatches`, keyed by task ID, carries the current dispatch summary from the same shared-lock snapshot as card state/revision: `dispatch_id`, `task_id`, `kind`, `epoch`, `state`, dispatch `revision`, original `created_at`/`confirm_by`, `age_seconds`, and durable `accepted`/`completed` receipts with card revisions and applicable delivery/disposition references.
